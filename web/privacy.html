Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Postie is a native macOS Gmail client. It runs entirely on your Mac and connects directly to Google's servers. There is no Postie server, and the developer does not receive, collect, or store your data.

Google user data

When you sign in, Postie requests these Gmail permissions:

  • gmail.modify to read your mail, to archive, trash, star, and mark mail as read or unread, and to save and delete your drafts
  • gmail.send to send mail you compose
  • gmail.settings.basic to read your signature so it can be added to new messages. Postie never changes your Gmail settings
  • contacts.readonly and contacts.other.readonly to read your Google contacts and the people you have written to, so the composer can suggest addresses. Postie never changes your contacts

Your mail is used only to display it in the app and to perform the actions you request. Postie never permanently deletes mail; Trash is Gmail's normal, recoverable Trash.

Storage

Sign-in credentials for each account you connect are stored in your Mac's Keychain. Mail you have loaded and your contacts are cached in a local database on your Mac. Removing an account in Postie's settings deletes its credentials and its cached mail. You can also revoke Postie's access at any time in your Google Account settings.

Sharing

Postie does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone. Your data is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop or train AI or machine learning models. Postie's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
